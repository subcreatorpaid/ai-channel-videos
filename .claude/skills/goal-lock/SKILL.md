# Goal lock

## Locked goal
**1,000 subscribers + 4,000 public watch hours by 8 Feb 2027** (set 10 Nov 2026). The owner changes it by saying "new goal lock: ...".
Repeat the goal at the top of every check.

## The five tests
1. **Hidden** - is it a job or process people rely on but rarely see (overnight maintenance, sanitation, logistics, building operations)?
2. **Twist** - is there one counterintuitive fact you can say in a single line?
3. **Scale** - a big number, money or system behind it that holds 10-12 minutes as a story (4-5 acts), not padding?
4. **Demand** - outliers or search demand in the last 90 days (`brain/competitors/`, vidIQ)?
5. **On brand** - drawable in our flat 2D style, sourceable from primary sources, respectful to workers, nothing from the never-list.

## Output (exactly this shape)
```
GOAL: 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours by 8 Feb 2027
IDEA: <one line>
VERDICT: On goal | One fix away | Off goal
TESTS: hidden ✓/✗ · twist ✓/✗ · scale ✓/✗ · demand ✓/✗ · on brand ✓/✗
WHY: <two sentences max>
FIX: <the one change, or "none needed">
SUGGESTED TITLE: <one line, if on goal or after the fix>
```
Views alone are not the goal; a viral video for the wrong audience is Off goal. Never soften a verdict.
