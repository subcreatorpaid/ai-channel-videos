# Script QA (Sonnet)

Check `videos/<slug>/script.md` and return `PASS` or a numbered fix list (fix it in place when you wrote the script):
1. **Cold open**: time and place, staggering fact, ironic reversal, "This is the story of..." inside 30 s; it matches the title/thumbnail promise.
2. **Length**: 1,500-1,700 words; sentences mostly 8-11 words. **Keyword** spoken in the first 30 s.
3. **Structure**: 4-5 named acts; each ends on a reversal; a turn every 60-90 s; the big answer at about 70-75%; every loop opened is closed.
4. **People**: one vignette, one worker line per act (short), one modern comparison; workers treated with respect.
5. **Sources**: every number, date and claim is in `sources.md`; no `[CHECK]` left; no accusatory framing of real people or companies.
6. **Originality**: no lines or structure lifted from another channel; the angle is new or clearly better than the outliers in `brief.md`.
7. **Voice**: nothing from the never-list in `brain/brand.md`; no "welcome back", "smash like", padding.
8. **Scenes**: a `[SCENE: ...]` cue after every line; every illustration listed.
9. **Ending**: callback + 3-6 word moral + "which hidden job next?" ask.
