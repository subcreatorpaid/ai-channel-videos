# Topic check (Sonnet, cheap)

For one topic, write 6 lines into the idea's `evidence` in `brain/ideas.json` (or reply with them when asked directly):
1. **Keyword**: the phrase people search; vidIQ `vidiq_keyword_research` volume + competition (or "no data").
2. **Outliers**: 1-3 videos on it from the last 90 days with views well above their channel's average (`vidiq_outliers` / `vidiq_youtube_search`).
3. **Twist**: the one-line counterintuitive fact, and whether a primary source backs it.
4. **Gap**: what those videos miss (no story, no workers' view, outdated, padded, no scale).
5. **Drawable**: can we tell it in flat 2D scenes (places, workers, machines, maps) without real footage?
6. **Verdict**: pass / weak / fail + one reason.
Fail = no twist, no demand and no outliers, can't be sourced, or would need restricted-site or how-to-break-in content. Max 3 vidIQ credits per topic.
