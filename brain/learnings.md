# Learnings (what works on this channel)

Rules learned from our own numbers (48-hour and 7-day reviews) and from research. Each line: date, rule, evidence. Newest first.
Until we have our own data, start from the benchmarks in `.claude/skills/youtube/references/` (algorithm, retention, thumbnail CTR guides).

- 2026-11-10 | Capital Case outliers = a household name or system + a counterintuitive twist, or breaking news "explained like you're 5"; abstract finance topics stall at ~5k views. | brain/research/2026-11-10-capital-case-blueprint.md
- 2026-11-10 | In the niche, worker-life and "Why X does Y" titles produce outliers 25-360x the channel median (Truck Culture 2M, Rig Insider 306k); one strong title beats volume. | brain/competitors/2026-11-10-hidden-workers-channels.md
- 2026-11-10 | Viewers call out typos, map errors and look-alike art fast (Capital Case comments): proofread every on-screen word, keep our own art style. | same
- 2026-11-10 | Starting rules from the first business: thumbnail before script, hook confirms the title promise in the first sentence, a re-hook every 60-90 s, storyboard before any render. | Sub-Creator Paid system
