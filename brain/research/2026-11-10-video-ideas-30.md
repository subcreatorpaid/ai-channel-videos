# 30 video ideas: hidden workers and processes (2026-11-10)

**Evidence used:**
- 5,345 niche videos pulled on Hammad's PC on 2026-11-10 (38 queries, YouTube API plus yt-dlp).
- Google Trends for the US over 12 months (pytrends).
- News checked on the web.

Raw lists are in `C:\Users\hamma\Documents\yt-research\ideas\` (A_top40.txt is the trending list, B_top40.txt the evergreen one, trends.json the Trends data).

**Note on the brief:** your message asked about celebrities, movies, award shows and talk shows. Those don't fit this niche. Google Trends shows "behind the scenes" searches are taken over by film and TV, and they're falling. So here "trending" means news events, seasonal spikes and recent outlier videos inside the niche.

## What the data says

- **Recent outliers come from new faceless channels under 15k subscribers.** For example:
  - The Navy Way (2k subs) got 1.52M views in 28 days with "How Container Ship Captains EAT, SHOWER & SLEEP".
  - Ground Truth (1k subs) got 366k views on a salt-mine video in 10 days.
  - Aviation Decoded (2k subs) got 175k views with "What Really Happens at an Airport Between 2 AM and 5 AM?".
- **Title patterns that win:**
  - "What really happens..."
  - "How X eat, sleep & shower"
  - "Why nobody can..."
  - "Every level of working at X"
  - "The hidden ... of"
  - Most winning videos run 15 to 25 minutes.
- **Hot right now:**
  - Crew life on giant ships. Cruise, cargo and rig videos had 5 of the top 10 outliers.
  - Truckers in blizzards.
  - Airports after hours.
  - Linemen. Searches rise every autumn and winter and are rising now.
  - Mines and the underground.
  - Holiday peak season. UPS "every level" got 51x its sub count.
- **News hooks:**
  - The US grid emergency order across 13 states in October 2026 ([Fox News, 13 Oct 2026](https://www.foxnews.com/us/trump-energy-department-issues-emergency-order-prevent-blackouts-13-states-unusual-october-heat)).
  - ATC staffing and incident news, which drives spikes in air traffic control searches.
  - Searches for "sanitation worker" peaked in October 2026.
  - Searches for "how does it work" are at a 12-month high.
- **Evergreen:**
  - Garbage trucks and where trash goes (91.5M views; Business Insider's NYC trash video got 13.5M).
  - Factory "how it's made" (tens of millions of views).
  - Cruise ships, container ships, mining, air traffic control, sewers and landfills.
  - Dangerous jobs and pay ladders.

## Series to scale to more uploads a week

| Series | Ideas | How it repeats |
|---|---|---|
| **Every Level** | 5, 14, 20 | Any job that has a ladder: UPS, USPS, airport, port, hotel, cruise. It's the most repeatable format and the data backs it (UPS 51x, lineman 25x). |
| **Life Below Deck** | 1, 8, 19, 29 | Cruise, cargo, rig, aircraft carrier, ferry, research vessel. It's the strongest current cluster. |
| **The Night Shift** | 2, 12, 23, 27 | "What really happens at [place] between [time] and [time]": stadium, hospital, subway, supermarket, hotel. |
| **Follow the Waste** | 3, 6, 15, 26 | Trash, sewage, recycling, e-waste, food waste. Evergreen and global. |
| **Extreme Shift** | 4, 7, 21 | Seasonal work in extreme conditions: blizzards, storms, heatwaves, floods. Plan it around the calendar. |
| **Jobs Nobody Wants** | 22, 24, 25 | Dangerous or dirty jobs told through stats and one worker's story. |
| **Explained Like You're 5** | 10 | One news-peg video a month, like Capital Case's Hormuz video. |

**How to scale:**
- Run 2 to 3 series in rotation: one long video a week from each picked series, plus Shorts cut from each video's best twist.
- Start with **Every Level**, **Life Below Deck** and **The Night Shift**, because they have the most proof.
