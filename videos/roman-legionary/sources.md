# Sources and accuracy notes: What If You Were Born As a Roman Legionary?

The hero (born AD 99 in southern Gaul, enlisted AD 117, discharged AD 142), his family, Brica, the tent-mates and the night raid are
hypothetical. Every dated or numbered fact below was checked on the pages listed (28 Nov 2026).

| Fact in the script | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Legionaries had to be freeborn citizens; recruits ideally 18-23; probatio (status + fitness check), then the sacramentum oath | [World History Encyclopedia: Roman Legionary](https://www.worldhistory.org/Roman_Legionary/), [Wikipedia: Legionary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legionary) |
| Auxiliaries received citizenship after 25 years | [Wikipedia: Imperial Roman army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Roman_army) |
| 25 years' service in practice (20 + 5 in reserve from AD 5) | [Wikipedia: Imperial Roman army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Roman_army) |
| Pay 300 denarii a year after Domitian; deductions for food, boots, kit | [Wikipedia: Legionary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legionary), [UNRV: Masada pay record](https://www.unrv.com/forum/topic/18872-roman-payslip-confirms-soldiers-complaint/) |
| 20 Roman miles in 5 summer hours at the military step | [Wikipedia: Military step](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_step), [Vegetius (UChicago)](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/encyclopaedia_romana/gladiators/vegetius.html) |
| Pack roughly 20-40 kg; "Marius' mules" nickname dates from c. 100 BC | [Wikipedia: Marian reforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marian_reforms) |
| 8 men per contubernium; 80 fighting men in a century | [Wikipedia: Contubernium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contubernium_(Roman_army_unit)) |
| Hadrian emperor 117; Wall begun 122, 80 Roman miles, built by three legions in about six years; milecastles and turrets | [Wikipedia: Hadrian's Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadrian%27s_Wall) |
| VI Victrix arrived in Britain c. 119-122, based at York | [Wikipedia: Legio VI Victrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legio_VI_Victrix) |
| Socks, sandals and two pairs of underpants letter (Tab. Vindol. 346, c. AD 104-120) | [Roman Inscriptions of Britain: Tab. Vindol. 346](https://romaninscriptionsofbritain.org/inscriptions/TabVindol346) |
| Soldiers could not legally marry until AD 197; unofficial families common | [Wikipedia: Legionary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legionary) |
| Hadrian died 138; Antonine Wall (turf) begun 142, abandoned within about 20 years | [Wikipedia: Hadrian's Wall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hadrian%27s_Wall) |
| Retirement: honesta missio and 3,000 denarii (about ten years' pay) | [Wikipedia: Veteran (Roman history)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veteran_(Roman_history)) |

Not separately sourced, phrased loosely on purpose: the birthday invitation (the Vindolanda letter of Claudia Severa, c. AD 100),
the vine-wood stick of centurions, lead identity tags, the funeral deduction, centurial building stones on the Wall.
Armour on screen: mail and segmented plate both appear (both were in use in the 2nd century).
